# Roadmap and review gates

Relative weeks begin when the project maintainer confirms kickoff and member availability. No calendar deadlines or procurement commitments have been assumed.

| Milestone | Proposed timing | Deliverables and exit gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| M0 Kickoff | Start | Confirm the three-subteam scope, roster information, access and team review routes; personal allocation remains deferred |
| M1 Requirements | Weeks 1-2 | Inventory, operating envelope, pressure/gas-load targets, interfaces and requirement-to-verification matrix; the project maintainer accepts a sourced baseline |
| M2 Design Review | Weeks 3-4 | Revisioned CAD, port schedule, external-pressure analysis, pump/conductance reasoning, controls concept, BOM and risks; interdisciplinary review resolves critical unknowns |
| M3 Procurement Readiness | Week 5, conditional | Approved drawings/specifications, quotes, budget scope, lead times, acceptance plan and purchasing authorization |
| M4 Commissioning | TBD | Receipt inspection, authorized installation, reviewed procedures, calibration and documented acceptance runs |
| Research and media | Continuous | Reviewed source notes, reproducible figures, contribution log, rights-cleared footage and release drafts |

Manufacturing, shipping, integration and acceptance durations depend on confirmed designs, suppliers, facility access and review. A five-week learning/design/procurement roadmap is not a promise of a commissioned chamber.

## Planning dependencies

Inventory and requirements precede the interface freeze. Loads and geometry precede FEA. Actual instrument manuals precede wiring release. Reviewed drawings and authorization precede orders. Approved procedures, facility permissions and calibrated instruments precede hardware runs.

## Budget boundary

The previously discussed USD 1,300 is a proposed chamber fabrication scope: metalwork, welding, polishing and helium leak testing. It is not an approved total-system budget. Pumps, gauges, valves, feedthroughs, controls, shipping and facility work require separate confirmed estimates and approval.

## Weekly review questions

What evidence was accepted? Who is blocked and who will help? Which interface changed? What is the next reviewable deliverable for each subteam? What must be decided before the next milestone?
