# Team structure and help routes

Project lead: the project maintainer. Current planning contains team-level deliverables only. No personal task owner, subteam membership, coordinator appointment or personal deadline is assigned.

## Three subteams

| Subteam | Scope | Main interfaces |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Diagnostics | Measurement requirements, diagnostic methods, calibration, validation plans and data quality | Sensor/electrical signals with Electronics & Control; access and mounting with Hardware Design & Vacuum Chamber |
| Electronics & Control | Electronics architecture, I/O, wiring, PCB requirements, controls, interlocks and data-acquisition interfaces | Measurement needs with Diagnostics; feedthroughs, mounting and equipment interfaces with Hardware Design & Vacuum Chamber |
| Hardware Design & Vacuum Chamber | Chamber CAD, structure, materials, ports, fabrication, vacuum hardware, pumping and mechanical integration | Diagnostic access with Diagnostics; electrical and control interfaces with Electronics & Control |

Cross-team work is tagged with every affected subteam and marked Cross-team. Analysis and validation are shared responsibilities within these three groups, not a separate fourth subteam.

## Membership privacy

Personal rosters, signup interests, previous memberships, contact details and individual assignments are excluded from this repository. Use the team's restricted collaboration space for membership administration.

## Questions and review

Ask in the relevant subteam discussion thread with the task link, attempted steps, evidence and help needed. Affected subteams review shared interfaces; the project maintainer routes unresolved questions. No individual coordinator or personal assignment is established here.

See the [team task register](team-tasks.md) for team-level deliverables and dependencies.
