# SRC-0002: Conductance and effective pumping speed

Status: PARTIALLY READ. Source: [Leybold Vacuum Fundamentals](https://www.leybold.com/en-be/knowledge/vacuum-fundamentals/fundamental-physics-of-vacuum/how-to-calculate-vacuum-conductance). Checked 2026-10-16. Citation key: `leybold_vacuum_conductance`.

Reading scope: the explanation connecting chamber pumping speed, pump inlet speed and the intervening gas-flow path. A pump's catalog inlet speed cannot by itself establish effective speed at the chamber. Conductance estimates depend on geometry, gas and flow regime.

Use in this project: prepare the gas-load/conductance assumptions sheet before selecting a pump or claiming a pumpdown result. Actual geometry, gas, operating pressure and component data remain UNKNOWN. No project calculation or pump choice is validated by this reading note.
